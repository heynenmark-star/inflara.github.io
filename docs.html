Protocol Principles

Inflara is designed around transparent, predefined monetary rules, publicly verifiable smart contracts and constrained governance.

CPI is used as a monetary-policy input. It does not establish a fixed price, guaranteed redemption value or guaranteed purchasing-power outcome for INFL.

Live Phase One Mechanics

  • Network: Inflara is deployed on Arbitrum One.
  • Policy timing: Daily epochs use a fixed mint amount established from a 30-day policy snapshot.
  • CPI policy: CPI is applied through predefined policy bands. CPI changes take effect at the next policy snapshot rather than changing the daily mint during the active period.
  • Staking: Staking rewards are capped at an annualized 8.5% of total staked. Any staking allocation above that cap is redirected to the Treasury.
  • Governance: Phase One administrative control is held by a 2-of-3 Safe multisig.
