Protocol Principles

Inflara is designed around transparent, predefined monetary rules, publicly verifiable smart contracts and constrained governance.

CPI is used as a monetary-policy input. It does not establish a fixed price, guaranteed redemption value or guaranteed purchasing-power outcome for INFL.
